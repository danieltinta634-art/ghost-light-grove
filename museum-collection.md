# Alastair's Museum — Cursed Artifact Collection

> Reference doc for Core Loop objects. See `characters/alastair.md` for the
> character himself and the "help him, he helps you" framing.

## The System (✅ canon, decided 2026-09-21)

- **Scale:** Stardew-scale, ~80–100 artifacts total.
- **Sourcing:** found across **all core loops** — farming, foraging,
  fishing, and exploring (Deep Woods + caves) — matching Stardew's
  artifact-spot/digging model. No single loop is favored.
- **Why donate:** Alastair's curse is an unnaturally long life that cursed
  artifacts sustain. He tells the player this openly, up front — it's the
  pitch for the whole mechanic, not a mystery to uncover. Every donation
  has a small personal stake for him, not just completionist decor-hunting.
- **Rewards:** donation milestones unlock **décor/furniture** (matches
  Stardew's Gunther rewards — rugs, statues, display cases, etc.).
- **Full-collection capstone:** ❓ open — not decided yet.
- **Categories (✅ locked):** four thematic sub-collections, browsable as
  separate tabs/cases in the museum, mixing tone across the whole set
  rather than tying a category to one source or one mood.

## Categories

### 1. Haunted Household Objects
Everyday domestic items gone wrong — the stuff of a cozy-gothic ghost
story, found in a grandmother's attic. Melancholy-to-spooky-but-gentle
tone.

- **The Weeping Mirror** — *"Cries real tears at dusk. Cleaning it is a
  two-person job — one to wipe, one to hold the bucket."* (forage, Woods)
- **Grandfather's Grandfather Clock** — *"Runs backwards exactly one hour
  a week. No one has ever caught it in the act."* (farming, found in a
  field)
- **The Never-Empty Teapot** — *"Always half-full of the same lukewarm
  tea. We do not know what kind. We have stopped asking."* (fishing, river)
- **Sympathy Candlestick** — *"Lights itself whenever someone nearby
  starts crying. Extremely considerate. Extremely unsettling."* (forage,
  swamp)
- **The Argument Rug** — *"Two people standing on it will disagree about
  something within four minutes. Tested extensively. Do not recommend."*
  (Deep Woods)
- **Hungry Coat Hook** — *"Coats hung on it are returned slightly
  chewed. It has never been caught doing this either."* (forage, town
  outskirts)

### 2. Occult / Ritual Items
Deliberate magic — things people *made* to do something, whether it
worked as intended or not. Leans more overtly witchy/ceremonial.

- **Chalk Circle (Portable)** — *"Folds up small enough for a pocket.
  Comes with a laminated instruction card, most of which has worn off."*
  (caves)
- **The Almost-Right Talisman** — *"Clearly meant to ward off something.
  We are not sure it wards off the RIGHT something."* (Deep Woods)
- **Scrying Bowl, Cracked** — *"Still works. Just shows you everything
  sideways."* (fishing, deep water)
- **A Single Ritual Candle, Unused** — *"Whatever it was for, someone
  changed their mind at the last minute. We'd like to know why."*
  (forage, graveyard-adjacent)
- **Binding Twine (Spool)** — *"Ordinary-looking. Extremely difficult to
  cut. We keep it far from the good scissors."* (farming)
- **The Third-Best Grimoire** — *"Missing its cover, its first ten pages,
  and apparently its sense of danger — the surviving spells are all
  fairly mundane."* (caves)

### 3. Cursed Relics & Heirlooms
Old, personal, historically weighty — objects with a family or a name
attached, even if the name is lost. More tragic/dramatic register.

- **The Locket With No Face** — *"The portrait inside has worn away to
  nothing. We like to imagine it was someone important."* (forage,
  manor grounds)
- **A Soldier's Unsent Letter** — *"Sealed, addressed, never delivered.
  We have chosen not to open it. Some things are still theirs."*
  (Deep Woods)
- **The Second Wedding Ring** — *"Matches no known first ring. The
  mystery is not who wore it — it's who it was waiting for."* (fishing)
- **Ancestral Cane (Unclaimed)** — *"Beautifully carved, clearly
  well-loved, and utterly without provenance. Someone leaned on this for
  a long time."* (farming, old fields)
- **The Monkey's Paw** — *"Yes, THAT one. Or one very much like it. We
  do not test these things twice."* (caves)
- **Portrait of a Stranger** — *"The eyes follow you. So does the rest
  of the painting, if you're not looking directly at it."* (manor
  grounds)

### 4. Joke / Prank Curses
Petty, silly, low-stakes magic — cursed in the most inconvenient possible
way rather than anything dangerous. Comic relief tier, keeps the museum
from being wall-to-wall melancholy.

- **The Sneezing Snuffbox** — *"Cursed to make anyone who opens it sneeze
  exactly three times. We've counted."* (farming)
- **Untieable Shoelace** — *"Ties itself into a double knot within one
  hour of being untied. We keep it on display already double-knotted, out
  of respect."* (forage, town)
- **The Overly Honest Mirror Shard** — *"Will comment, unprompted, on
  your outfit. Its opinions are not always kind."* (fishing)
- **Backwards-Talking Bell** — *"Rings in reverse. We are still not sure
  what that means for a bell, but it's very distinctive."* (Deep Woods)
- **The Petty Curse Jar** — *"Contains, according to the peeling label,
  'one (1) minor inconvenience.' We have not opened it. We are not going
  to."* (caves)
- **Chair That's Always Slightly Wobbly** — *"On every surface. In every
  orientation. We have given up and named it a feature."* (farming)

# Alastair's Museum — Cursed Artifact Collection

> Reference doc for Core Loop objects. See `characters/alastair.md` for the
> character himself and the "help him, he helps you" framing.

## The System (✅ canon, decided 2026-09-21)

- **Scale:** Stardew-scale, ~80–100 artifacts total.
- **Sourcing:** found across **all core loops** — farming, foraging,
  fishing, and exploring (Deep Woods + caves) — matching Stardew's
  artifact-spot/digging model. No single loop is favored.
- **Why donate:** Alastair's curse is an unnaturally long life that cursed
  artifacts sustain. He tells the player this openly, up front — it's the
  pitch for the whole mechanic, not a mystery to uncover. Every donation
  has a small personal stake for him, not just completionist decor-hunting.
- **Rewards:** donation milestones unlock **décor/furniture** (matches
  Stardew's Gunther rewards — rugs, statues, display cases, etc.).
- **Full-collection capstone:** ❓ open — not decided yet.
- **Categories (✅ locked):** four thematic sub-collections, browsable as
  separate tabs/cases in the museum, mixing tone across the whole set
  rather than tying a category to one source or one mood.

## Categories

### 1. Haunted Household Objects
Everyday domestic items gone wrong — the stuff of a cozy-gothic ghost
story, found in a grandmother's attic. Melancholy-to-spooky-but-gentle
tone.

*(Items TBD.)*

### 2. Occult / Ritual Items
Deliberate magic — things people *made* to do something, whether it
worked as intended or not. Leans more overtly witchy/ceremonial.

- **The Unsolved Puzzle Box** — *"Ornate, heavy, and — as far as we can
  tell — unopenable. We have theories about what happens if someone
  finally manages it. We have chosen not to test them."* (Deep Woods)

### 3. Cursed Relics & Heirlooms
Old, personal, historically weighty — objects with a family or a name
attached, even if the name is lost. More tragic/dramatic register.

- **The Monkey's Paw** — *"Yes, THAT one. Or one very much like it. We
  do not test these things twice."* (caves)

### 4. Joke / Prank Curses
Petty, silly, low-stakes magic — cursed in the most inconvenient possible
way rather than anything dangerous. Comic relief tier, keeps the museum
from being wall-to-wall melancholy.

- **Battered Hockey Mask** — *"Pulled from a lake no one will name.
  Wearing it makes you walk slightly slower than everyone around you.
  We've had to ask people to stop testing this indoors."* (fishing)
- **"Lucky" Rabbit's Foot** — *"Advertised as good luck. We regret to
  inform whoever carved that sign that the rabbit did not, historically,
  have particularly good luck."* (forage, Woods)

Alice is the Grove's historian and lore-voice — the library is the town's
archive, and bringing her inscriptions or records to research is how the
game's mystery deliveries run. A marriage candidate — the composed
intellectual of the pool. ✅ She's a reformed vampire, in recovery **by her
own choice**: she sought out Myra, the one person in the Grove who helps
monsters instead of hunting them, to get her bloodlust under control. Myra
took her in, and Alice has lived at the sanctuary ever since, tending the
vampire bats — a reformed vampire caring for the little ones.

✅ **Emotional core (canon):** Hazel's suppressant tonic manages her blood
cravings — management, not cure; she's still a vampire, and needs the brew
ongoing. That dependency is what lets her live among people and run the
library at all. She shares a quiet kinship with Briar, the other afflicted
one in town — both manage a condition, both depend on Hazel, a natural
unspoken bond between the two who need the tonics.

Clementine is Victor's daughter — a little goth girl with Wednesday Addams
energy, one of only two living humans in a town built as a refuge for the
strange. ✅ That framing is the whole hook: what does a child make of
growing up in a place like this, raised by a father who argues with himself
about the machine he runs?

✅ **The "arguing with himself" is literal (canon):** Victor is involuntarily
possessed, on and off, by a spirit — a side-effect of his own ghost-light
work (see `characters/victor.md` and `story-spoiler-doc.md`). Clementine
**knows, and is completely unbothered by it** — "spirit dad" barely
registers against her baseline deadpan. It's a good, concrete answer to
"what does this kid make of growing up here": not fear, not confusion,
just another Tuesday.

❓ **Open thread (canon-adjacent):** the story doc names "Victor & Clementine's
arrival wound" as a beat still to be filled in — what they needed when the
Grove drew them, and whether that wound is hers to carry too, or purely
his.

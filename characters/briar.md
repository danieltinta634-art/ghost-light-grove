Briar runs the building/construction vendor slot — a grump with a soft
center, and a marriage candidate. ✅ He relies on Hazel's suppressant tonic
to manage his werewolf affliction day-to-day, but the **Full Moon overrides
it** every month regardless — even the best witch can't out-brew the moon —
so he's absent every Full Moon, felt rather than seen. Management, not
cure: he's still a werewolf, and always will be.

✅ **Emotional core (canon):** he shares a quiet kinship with Alice, the
town's other afflicted resident — neither chose their condition, both
depend on Hazel, a natural unspoken bond between the two who need the
tonics. ✅ **The running bit:** he drinks at Benny's bar most nights and has
never once laughed at one of his jokes — the town's longest-running,
gentlest feud, entirely from Benny's side of the bar.

## Relationships
- **Hazel** — the tonic that manages his affliction comes from her; a

- **Alice** — quiet kinship as the town's other afflicted resident.
- **Benny** — the world's least receptive audience for Benny's act, night
  after night, without ever meaning any harm by it.

# Rowan — Art & Assets
*(Reference only. ✅ core direction confirmed 2026-09-20.)*

**Appearance (✅ confirmed):**
Leans into the **Herne-the-Hunter/Green Man** mythological lineage rather
than a purely plant-based dryad look. **Antlers** (branch-like, not deer-
realistic) and a **foliage crown** woven with leaves/vines. **Bark-textured
skin** — visible bark-like grain and texture, not smooth. **Imposing
build** — broad, sturdy, mythic presence. **Soft, subtly glowing eyes** —
not blazing or harsh, a gentle supernatural tell. Wears **simple woodsman/
caretaker clothes** (a tunic, wraps, roughspun earthy fabric) rather than
foliage-as-clothing — grounds the mythic silhouette in someone who does
physical work tending the Woods. Overall read: mature, steady, settled —
an older, calm energy rather than a youthful one.

**Sizes (once defined):** portrait 64×64 (head/shoulders — antlers,
foliage crown, and glowing eyes should all read clearly at this scale) ·
overworld sprite 16×32 (at his home — a large ancient tree in the Woods) ·
concept full-body any size.

**Open:** exact palette (bark tone, foliage color, glow color) · precise
antler shape/size relative to sprite scale · his home tree's exterior
design (how obviously "lived-in" it looks from outside).

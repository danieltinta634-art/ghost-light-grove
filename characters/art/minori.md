# Minori — Art & Assets
*(Reference only. ✅ core direction confirmed 2026-09-19; finer details still open.)*

**Appearance (✅ confirmed):**
Fox-eared/tailed kitsune, **white/pale fur** (a byakko-style, sacred/
celestial kitsune variant in folklore) with **vermillion/red accents**
echoing torii-gate and shrine color — leans into authentic Inari-shrine
iconography rather than a moon motif (kitsune aren't canonically lunar).
**Foxfire** (a soft, ghostly glow — blue-white or pale flame) is her
signature magical visual and fits the perpetual-night setting naturally.
Dresses **casual/practical, not ceremonial** — everyday clothes with small
fox/shrine accents (a charm, a sash), deliberately undercutting the
solemnity a shrine-keeper "should" have, matching her sharp-witted,
always-one-step-ahead personality. Playful and mischievous expression and
posture, light on her feet. **Tail count:** unresolved, pure visual
flavor — pick whatever reads best at sprite scale (folklore ties tail
count to age/power, but that meaning is explicitly NOT being used here).

**Sizes (once defined):** portrait 64×64 (head/shoulders, mischievous
grin) · overworld sprite 16×32 (at her shrine, on the manor-to-town path) ·
concept full-body any size.

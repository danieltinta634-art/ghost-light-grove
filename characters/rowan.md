Rowan is the pool's elegant, nurturing one — calm, rooted, graceful, a male
tree/forest spirit named for the rowan tree. ✅ Positioned as a slow-blooming,
earthy romance.

✅ **Role (canon, decided 2026-09-20):** tends the Woods — growth, forage,
tree health — with **no shop and no mechanical hook**, a purely social/
flavor role like several Stardew candidates (Leah, Elliott, Haley) who work
fine without one. No art/craft hobby attached either (woodcarving and other
art-form options were considered and dropped).

✅ **Personality (canon, decided 2026-09-20):** layer a **mature, steady,
older, reserved-but-caring energy** (an "older Harvey" vibe — personality
and age, not his profession) onto the existing "calm, rooted, graceful."
Settled and grown-up rather than youthful, without taking on any
doctor-adjacent function (Hazel already covers healing/apothecary).

✅ **Appearance (canon, decided 2026-09-20):** leans into the **Herne-the-
Hunter/Green Man** side of the archetype rather than a purely plant-based
look — antlers and a foliage crown, bark-textured skin, an imposing build.
Soft, subtly glowing eyes. Wears simple woodsman/caretaker clothes rather
than going bark-as-clothing — grounds the mythic silhouette in someone who
does physical work.

✅ **Home (canon, decided 2026-09-20):** lives inside/as part of a large,
ancient tree in the Woods — more overtly magical/fairy-tale than a plain
cabin, matching the antlered mythic weight of his design.

Tobias is the Grove's civic heart — mayor, festival host, and the town's
"you belong here" made into a person. ✅ **He carries his own head around:**
sets it on the podium for speeches, tucks it under his arm, occasionally
misplaces it — a running gag played completely straight, against a
dignified, warm, ceremonial demeanor. He runs the festivals, one per tide,
and his office sits on the town square connected to a stable housing his
horse **Maple** — he still rides, the one detail of the old Headless
Horseman legend he never let go of.

🔶 **Story role (proposed):** likely the **first** resident the Grove ever
drew — the one who's welcomed every arrival since. If true, every other
character's "how they got here" story eventually points back to Tobias
opening the door.

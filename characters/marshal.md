Marshal is a young adult swamp monster who came to the Grove by water — ✅
he traveled the river system to reach the town and settled in the waterways
beneath it, in the sewer entrance under the town-square bridge. The
connected waterways (sewer → river → swamp) are his native habitat, so he
commutes down to Hazel's swamp easily; it's his comfort zone as much as
hers. A marriage candidate — the shy sweetheart of the pool.

light reaches out over. Not yet confirmed as his origin story, just the

Minori — "harvest" / "bearing of fruit" — is the pool's playful trickster:
mischievous, teasing, flirtatious, the light and charming foil to the pool's
more guarded types. ✅ She's the cast's one deliberately non-western
mythological figure (everyone else draws on European gothic folklore —
vampire, werewolf, siren, dryad, ghost); her design leans **into** that
rather than blending it away.

✅ **Role (canon, decided 2026-09-19): shrine keeper, harvest luck.** Tends a
small shrine tied to crop fortune — leaning into authentic Inari-shrine
iconography (stone fox statues, offerings) rather than a generic
trickster/illusionist take, since kitsune are traditionally Inari's
messengers and Inari is the Shinto god of rice, agriculture, and
prosperity. No one else in the cast currently touches harvest-luck/crop-
fortune territory, so this is unclaimed ground for her. Her magic reads as
**foxfire and illusion** (her authentic mythological signature) rather than
anything moon-specific — kitsune aren't canonically lunar; that was an
earlier mischaracterization, corrected this session.

✅ **The shrine mechanic (canon, decided 2026-09-19):** leave a crop/farm
good as a daily offering → a small **general luck buff** for that day
(touches crop quality/yield, forage rarity, and fish rarity all a little,
rather than one targeted stat) — refreshable every day, like a Stardew
daily-luck check built around a ritual instead of a TV. The buff is
**stronger during Harvest-tide**, her namesake season, giving that tide
unique flavor beyond its crop-list swap. She keeps **no shop** — this is
her entire mechanical role, distinct from every other candidate, who all
double as vendors (Briar=building, Myra=livestock, Hazel=potions, etc.).
She **lives at the shrine**, sited on the manor-to-town path (near Cliff's
graveyard stairs) so the daily offering folds into the player's existing
commute rather than requiring a special trip. She also **assists Tobias
with festivals** generally (not just Harvest-tide's) — recurring support,
not a once-a-year spotlight.

✅ **Arrival (canon, decided 2026-09-19):** she's one of the outcasts
Dorian's amplified lighthouse song called to the Grove — giving her and
Dorian shared history that predates their "foil" dynamic. Her specific
need: outside the veil, she was **feared and hunted for her tricks** —
harmless mischief read as a threat, same pattern as Dorian's own exile as
a "hazardous" siren. Direct thematic parallel between the two of them.

✅ **Personality/voice (canon, decided 2026-09-19):** sharp-witted, quick
banter — always a step or two ahead in conversation, playful in a
competitive rather than purely gentle way. Talking to her feels like a
game she's already winning.

✅ **Foil to Dorian (canon, decided 2026-09-19):** she teases the brooding
artist, and — being a trick-spirit herself — is genuinely **immune to his
siren allure**. One of the only people in town who's always seen him as
just a guy, not the supernatural charisma. Combined with their shared
arrival history, this gives their dynamic real weight underneath the
banter, not just surface friction.

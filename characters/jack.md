Jack is the cornfield's scarecrow — and the town's one deliberate
non-mystery. ✅ **He is never seen moving, and yet he attends every
festival anyway.** Nobody in town remarks on it; the game never explains
it. It's a running gag, explicitly **not** a mystery to be solved — the
dusk report (the between-day summary of what changed overnight) is one of
the few places his position visibly shifts, "facing the house now," and
that's as far as it goes.

✅ **Design rule (canon):** Jack is on the short list of things the game
deliberately never answers, alongside the player's parents and possibly the
twins' in-life question. Do not fill this in.

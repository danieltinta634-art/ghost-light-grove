Myra is the Grove's monster rehabilitator — and the town's biggest subversion of
what that should mean. ✅ She carries a hunter's kit (Van Helsing's tools:
tracking, trapping, knowing every creature's weakness) and reads as one to
strangers — the gear, the scars, the confidence. The truth is the opposite:
she uses every one of those skills to rescue, never to kill. **The town's
monster hunter has never treated a monster as a monster.** She traps the
Grove's wild risen out of the Deep Woods, runs the sanctuary/ranch (vampire
bats, giant spiders, other eerie livestock — tended, collected from, raised),
and sells what the ranch produces. A marriage candidate — the bold,
confident-adventurer archetype of the pool.

✅ **Emotional core (canon):** Myra can read the risen. Most wild risen are
mindless, hostile husks, beyond reach — but rarely one is different: less
feral, a flicker behind the eyes, hesitation instead of a lunge. She is the
**only** one in the Grove who can tell, and those are the ones she brings to
Victor for ghost light therapy; the rest stay wild. That's why uplift is rare
— only a handful ever had the spark — and why she's irreplaceable: she sees
the person still inside the monster before anyone else can. She still
fights, and can drop a wild risen if she has to; she just won't unless
there's no other choice. **The most dangerous person in town, and the
gentlest.**

✅ **The rescue pipeline (canon, backstory only):** Cliff and Benny both came
through her — mindless when she found them in the Deep Woods, brought to
Victor, and uplifted into who they are now. It stays backstory flavour, not
a player-facing loop: Myra works upstream of the player, so what the reaper
fights in the Deep Woods are the genuine husks — no mind left to save, no
moral weight, no cross-purposes between the reaper and the rehabilitator.
✅ Alice came to her too, but for herself, not as a capture: she sought out
the one person in the Grove who helps monsters instead of hunting them, to
get her bloodlust under control. Myra took her in; Alice lives at the
sanctuary now, tending the vampire bats, in recovery by her own choice.

## Relationships
- **Alice** — took her in when Alice came seeking help; Alice still lives and

- **Victor** — the other half of the rescue pipeline: she finds the
  salvageable risen, he does the uplift. Professional, essential, ongoing.

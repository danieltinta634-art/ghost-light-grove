Hazel is the Grove's apothecary — and its second big subversion of a spooky
cliché. ✅ **Gentle, elderly, caring, grandmotherly, not gruff:** the eerie
witch's hut deep in the swamp hides a kind old woman brewing medicine for
whoever needs her. She's load-bearing, not background — her suppressant
tonics are what let two of the town's afflicted actually live their lives.

✅ **Emotional core (canon):** Hazel brews the tonics that calm Alice's
blood cravings and ease Briar's werewolf affliction — **management, not
cure**. Alice is still a vampire, Briar is still a werewolf; they need the
brews ongoing, which is exactly why Hazel is needed and irreplaceable. Even
her best work can't out-brew the Full Moon, which overrides Briar's tonic
every month regardless. She rarely leaves the swamp, so **Sabrina**, her
apprentice, carries the tonics to Alice and Briar as the apothecary's public
face — the reclusive witch brews, the sunny apprentice delivers.

🔶 **Mentor, not master (proposed framing):** her arc with Sabrina is warm
grandmother-and-granddaughter mentorship — she's teaching Sabrina to become
a witch in her own right, not keeping her an assistant forever.

## Relationships
- **Alice** — one of her two "regulars"; the tonic keeps Alice's cravings
  livable, and stable enough to live among people and run the library.
- **Briar** — her other regular; the tonic manages his affliction every day
  the Full Moon isn't in the sky.
- **Sabrina** — her apprentice, delivery-girl, and the public face of the
  apothecary. Teaching her to be as good a witch as she is.

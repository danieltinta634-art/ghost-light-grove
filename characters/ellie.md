Ellie is Benny's daughter and the bar's resident chaos — the "chaos twin" to
Kellie's responsible one. ✅ Like Kellie, she came from Victor's experiments,
uplifted alongside Benny; whether the three of them were family in life is
unknown, and possibly unknowable, since none of the uplifted keep memories
of who they were. What's certain is Benny is her father *now*, by choice,
and the bar is home — the twins run underfoot "helping" while he works the
room.

🔶 **Emotional core (proposed):** where Kellie manages, Ellie invents —
chaos as a form of joy, mess as a love language. She doesn't carry the
weight of the unanswered in-life question at all; as far as she's concerned
the family that's certain (Benny, Kellie, the bar) is the only one that
matters.

## Relationships
- **Kellie** — twin sister, the responsible half of the pair. Inseparable.
- **Benny** — father, in the only way that counts. He can't confirm they
  were family before the ghost light, and neither of them seems to need him
  to.

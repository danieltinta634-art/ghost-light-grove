Kellie is Benny's daughter and the bar's steady hand — the "responsible
twin" to Ellie's chaos. ✅ Like Ellie, she came from Victor's experiments,
uplifted alongside Benny; whether the three of them were family in life is
unknown, and possibly unknowable, since none of the uplifted keep memories
of who they were. What's certain is Benny is her father *now*, by choice,
and the bar is home — the twins run underfoot "helping" while he works the
room.

🔶 **Emotional core (proposed):** where Ellie invents, Kellie manages —
counting the till, minding her sister, quietly making sure the night runs
smoothly around her father's act. Not a killjoy, just the one who notices
what needs doing.

## Relationships
- **Ellie** — twin sister, the chaos half of the pair. Inseparable; she
  covers for Ellie without complaint.
- **Benny** — father, in the only way that counts. He can't confirm they
  were family before the ghost light, and neither of them seems to need him
  to.

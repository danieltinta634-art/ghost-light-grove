Lionel runs the town's everyday general store — the Pierre's-of-Pelican-Town
slot, selling seeds and staples. ✅ He's an **English archaeologist from
the 1800s**, Egyptology's golden age — the archaeologist who became the
artifact — and it shows in every word he says: Shakespearean gravitas, grand
weighty diction, narrating the price of turnip seeds like a tomb
inscription. ✅ Big bushy grey eyebrows and a bushy grey moustache burst out
from under his mummy bandages.

✅ **Design rule (canon):** his archaeology background is flavour and voice
only — never a mechanic. He's kept deliberately distinct from Alastair's
museum (cursed artifacts, donated not sold) and Alice's library
(lore/research): Lionel just runs the shop, magnificently.

Victor harvests ghost light — the pooled backlog of unreaped souls — from
the caves beneath his lab to power the town and suppress the wild risen,
banishing them to the Deep Woods. ✅ **He is a protector, not a culprit:**
he arrived *after* the player's grandfather left, found an untended source
and a town that needed it managed, and has been running it ever since. He's
blind to the one thing that matters most — the sun goddess's shrine, deeper
in the same cave system, which he's never found and doesn't know is there.
The player's wage, funded by ghost light, literally feeds his machine —
deliberate moral friction built into the economy.

✅ **Emotional core (canon):** Victor argues with himself. He uplifted the
rare salvageable risen — Cliff, Benny, the twins — using the same ghost
light therapy the town runs on, restoring a mind but never the old one.
That's public knowledge, and presumably a source of both pride and unease:
he can give someone a life back, just never *their* life back. Myra brings
him the ones with a spark left; he does the rest.

❓ **Open thread:** what he needed when the Grove drew him — did he arrive
already obsessed with the sky, or did that come after? The story doc frames
him and Clementine as "two living humans in a refuge for the strange,"
which implies an arrival wound neither of them has resolved.

## Relationships
- **Myra** — the other half of the rescue pipeline: she finds the
  salvageable risen, he does the uplift. Professional, essential, ongoing.
- **Cliff, Benny** (and the twins, Ellie & Kellie) — he gave them their
  minds back, though not their old ones. Whether he treats them as
  patients, creations, or just neighbors now is open.
- **Clementine** — his daughter; came to the Grove with him.

Sabrina — "Sab" — is Hazel's apprentice and the apothecary's public face: a
marriage candidate, and the pool's sunshine archetype. ✅ Bubbly, bright,
eager. Since Hazel rarely leaves the swamp, Sabrina is the one who actually
**carries the tonics to Alice and Briar** — running Hazel's deliveries and
errands all over town, which puts her in regular contact with most of the
cast.

✅ **Emotional core (canon):** her arc is learning to become as good a
witch as Hazel — apprentice becoming a witch in her own right, framed as
warm grandmother-and-granddaughter mentorship rather than a master
withholding knowledge. She's the reclusive witch's bright, moving half.

## Relationships

Wilcox is the manor's ghost butler — proper, snobby, utterly loyal, and the
first face the player meets. ✅ He's held the house alone for years, turning
away the whole town, until the player arrives carrying the family pendant;
he admits them the moment he sees it, then bows. He's static as the game's
tutorial NPC — the fixed point the farming/sleep/dusk-report loop is proven
against before anything else gets built.

✅ **Emotional core (canon):** Wilcox served the player's grandfather, the
manor's last resident — the one who bound the goddess beneath the caves,
walked away from the reaper's office, and left the Grove and his family
behind (why: still open). Wilcox never took another master in all the years
since, and never let anyone else through the door. The pendant is the
office's insignia, dormant until it crosses the veil; the moment it wakes,
spirits notice and Wilcox bows — not to the player, exactly, but to what
they're now carrying.

🔶 **The slow reveal (proposed structure, ✅ mechanic):** Wilcox "remembers
more" as the manor heals — each restored room unlocks a piece of his memory
alongside its facility, so his knowledge of the grandfather, the office, and
the goddess surfaces in step with the restoration meta-progression rather
than all at once.
